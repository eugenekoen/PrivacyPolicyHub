AutoLog Privacy Policy

Tax Mileage & Trip Logbook • Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

AutoLog ("we", "our", or "the App") is a local-first vehicle trip and mileage tracking application designed for global tax compliance (including USA IRS, Canada CRA, UK HMRC, European Union, and South Africa SARS logbook standards). Your privacy is extremely important to us. AutoLog stores all travel logs, location data, odometer records, saved places, and configuration settings locally on your device. AutoLog does not operate remote servers and does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information or location data.

2. Data We Access & How It Is Used

  • Location Data (Foreground and Background): AutoLog uses precise and approximate location coordinates (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, and ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION) to automatically trace trip routes, calculate mileage distance (in kilometers or miles), resolve start/end street addresses, and match saved location tags (e.g., Home, Office, Client). Background location tracking is activated ONLY when a trip is actively being recorded (indicated by a persistent foreground status bar notification).
  • Address Geocoding: Start and end GPS coordinates are converted into street addresses using Android's native on-device Geocoder (or directly via the Google Maps Geocoding API if you optionally configure your own API key in Settings).
  • Bluetooth Device State: AutoLog monitors Bluetooth connection states (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT and BLUETOOTH_SCAN) solely to detect when your phone connects to or disconnects from your designated vehicle's hands-free audio system to start and stop trip recording automatically.
  • Subscriptions & Payments (Google Play Billing): Subscription trials, localized currency pricing, and payments are processed securely by Google Play Billing. AutoLog only receives subscription status confirmation from the Google Play Store app and never accesses, collects, or stores your credit card, banking, or billing details.
  • Biometric Lock: If enabled, authentication uses Android's native BiometricPrompt system and OS keystore. AutoLog never accesses, stores, or transmits raw biometric credentials (fingerprints or facial recognition data).
  • Local Storage & Exports: All trip entries, odometer readings, and settings remain on your device in a local SQLite database and encrypted preferences. Exported CSV logbooks or JSON backups are generated locally and shared only when you explicitly invoke Android's native share sheet.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not operate remote servers or cloud databases, nor do we integrate third-party advertising, behavioral analytics, or user-tracking SDKs. Your data is never sold, rented, shared, or uploaded automatically.

4. Data Retention & Control

You retain 100% control over your data on your device. You may view, edit, reclassify, export (CSV/JSON), or permanently delete individual trip records and saved locations inside the app at any time. Uninstalling AutoLog or clearing the app's storage in Android Settings immediately and permanently removes all saved data from your device.

5. International Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, PIPEDA & POPIA)

Because AutoLog processes and stores your data exclusively on your local device without transmitting it to us, you have direct, immediate control over your statutory privacy rights:

  • European Economic Area (EEA) & United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR): Processing of location and trip data occurs strictly locally on your device based on your explicit consent (granting runtime permissions) and to perform the logbook functionality you request. You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), restriction, and data portability directly within the App at any time via the Edit, Delete, and Export CSV/Backup features, or by revoking permissions in Android Settings.
  • United States (CCPA / CPRA & State Privacy Laws): We do not "sell" or "share" your personal information or sensitive geolocation data as defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) or other U.S. state privacy laws, nor do we use geolocation data for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Canada (PIPEDA): All personal and trip mileage information remains under your local custody and control on your device. You may withdraw consent for automatic tracking at any time by disabling Bluetooth auto-start or revoking location permissions.
  • South Africa (POPIA): AutoLog complies with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) by ensuring data minimization and strict local-only storage under your sole control.

6. Children's Privacy

AutoLog is a tax and vehicle mileage utility intended for licensed drivers and adults. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

7. Required App Permissions

Permission Purpose
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION Calculates precise trip mileage distance and determines start/stop street addresses.
ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION Allows continuous automatic trip recording when driving with the screen off or while using navigation apps.
BLUETOOTH_CONNECT / BLUETOOTH_SCAN Detects your selected vehicle's hands-free Bluetooth connection to start and stop automatic trip logging.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION Displays an active recording notification in the Android system tray during live trip tracking.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Displays active trip status alerts and post-trip classification prompts (Business vs. Personal).
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS Prevents the Android OS from prematurely terminating the tracking service during long drives.
USE_BIOMETRIC Allows optional fingerprint or face unlock protection when opening your logbook.
