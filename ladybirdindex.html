1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Ladybird Connect ("Ladybird Connect", "we", "our", or "us"). Ladybird Connect is a multi-tenant childcare and preschool management platform designed for early childhood educators, preschool administrators, and authorized parents or legal guardians.

We take your privacy and the protection of children's data very seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, store, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you use our Android mobile application and connected cloud services.

By accessing or using Ladybird Connect, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Adult-Only Use & Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Ladybird Connect is intended exclusively for adult educators and verified adult parents or legal guardians.

  • No Direct Child Access: Children under 13 (or under the age of majority in your jurisdiction) are not permitted to register accounts, log into the application, or interact with our services directly.
  • Parental Consent: All child profile information (names, birth dates, classroom assignments, allergy directives, and daily care logs) is collected and recorded solely with verifiable parental consent obtained by the enrolled preschool or nursery.
  • Compliance with International Child Protection Laws: We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation for Kids (GDPR-K), and the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). We never collect personal data directly from children.

3. Information We Collect

A. Information Provided by Users and Educators

  • User Account Information: Full name, email address, contact phone number, secure password credentials (hashed and encrypted), designated role (School Administrator, Teacher, or Parent), and avatar preference.
  • School & Nursery Profiles: School name, principal name, contact email, phone number, operating city, classroom names, and room schedules.
  • Child Profile Records: First name, nickname, date of birth / age group category, classroom assignment, emergency contact details, dietary restrictions, allergy alerts, and special care directives.
  • Daily Care Logs & Observations: Routine daily logs authored by nursery educators, including bottle feeds (amounts in ml), food portions consumed, nap times and durations, diaper and potty training entries, child mood logs, tummy time sessions, sensory play, developmental milestones, supply restock alerts, and incident reports.
  • Parent & School Communications: Messages, notices, and calendar events posted to preschool classrooms or shared between verified parents and teachers.

B. Automatically Collected Information & Device Telemetry

  • Push Notification Device Tokens: We collect device Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration tokens exclusively to deliver instant, real-time push notifications regarding child logs, diaper/feed updates, emergency notes, and calendar announcements.
  • App Diagnostic Telemetry: Basic error logs and crash diagnostics to maintain app stability and verify cloud synchronization.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for legitimate childcare, educational, and operational purposes, including:

  • Preschool Management: Enabling teachers and administrators to organize classrooms, log daily care routines, track attendance, and record developmental progress.
  • Parental Communication: Providing verified parents and guardians with real-time updates and transparent peace of mind regarding their child's day.
  • Health & Safety: Alerting nursery staff to food allergies, medical precautions, and managing incident reports promptly.
  • Push Notifications: Transmitting immediate transactional alerts for feeding, diapering, emergency announcements, and calendar events.
  • Subscription & Licensing: Verifying school capacity and subscription tier status via Google Play.

We do NOT sell, rent, lease, or monetize personal information, parent contact details, or child data to third-party data brokers, marketers, or advertising networks.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

To deliver secure cloud infrastructure, authentication, and billing, we partner with industry-leading service providers who process data strictly under confidentiality agreements:

  • Supabase (PostgreSQL & Authentication):
    • Purpose: Secure, encrypted cloud database hosting, user authentication, and multi-tenant data synchronization.
    • Security: Industry-standard TLS encryption in transit, AES encryption at rest, and strict database Row Level Security (RLS) isolating records per tenant and parent.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM):
    • Purpose: Transmitting secure, encrypted push notifications to mobile devices.
  • Google Play In-App Billing:
    • Purpose: Secure processing of recurring school subscriptions and plan limits.
    • Payment Security: All financial transactions, credit card numbers, and banking details are processed directly by Google Play. Ladybird Connect does not collect, process, or store financial account details.

6. Data Security & Role-Based Access Controls

We employ rigorous physical, technical, and administrative safeguards to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure:

  • Tenant & Role-Based Isolation: Parents are strictly restricted to viewing only the records of their own explicitly linked children. Educators are restricted to children enrolled within their preschool.
  • Transport Layer Security: All communications between the mobile application and cloud servers are encrypted using modern HTTPS / TLS protocols.
  • Password Security: User passwords are encrypted with salted one-way hashes via Supabase Auth and are never stored in plaintext.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

In compliance with Google Play's User Data and Account Deletion policies, users and schools have complete control over their personal data:

  • Right to Access & Rectify: Parents and educators can review and update their profile details at any time within the app settings.
  • In-App Management: School administrators can delete individual child profiles, unlink parents, or remove staff accounts directly through the People Directory inside the app.
  • Account & Data Deletion Requests: You have the absolute right to request the permanent deletion of your account, linked children, and all historical care records. To request deletion:
    • Email: Send a request to eugenekoenn@gmail.com with the subject line "Account & Data Deletion Request", specifying your registered email address and school name.
    • Processing Time: All associated personal information, child records, and device tokens will be permanently purged from our active cloud databases within 30 days of verification.

8. Google Play Subscription Terms

  • School subscriptions are billed monthly in South African Rand (ZAR) via Google Play In-App Billing following the conclusion of any applicable free trial period.
  • Subscriptions automatically renew each month unless canceled at least 24 hours prior to the conclusion of the current billing cycle.
  • Users can manage or cancel their subscription at any time by navigating to: Google Play Store > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
