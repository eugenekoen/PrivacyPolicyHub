AutoLog Privacy Policy

Tax Mileage & Trip Logbook • Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

AutoLog ("we", "our", or "the App") is a local-first vehicle trip and mileage tracking application designed for tax compliance (including SARS logbook formats). Your privacy is extremely important to us. AutoLog stores all travel logs, location data, saved places, and configuration settings locally on your device. AutoLog does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information or location data on remote servers.

2. Data We Access & How It Is Used

  • Location Data (Foreground and Background): AutoLog uses precise location coordinates (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION) to automatically trace trip routes, calculate mileage distance, geocode start/end street addresses, and match saved location tags. Background location tracking is activated ONLY when a trip is active (indicated by an active foreground status bar notification).
  • Bluetooth Device State: AutoLog monitors Bluetooth connection states (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT / BLUETOOTH_SCAN) solely to detect when your phone connects to your vehicle's audio system to start and stop trip recording automatically.
  • Biometric Lock: If enabled, authentication uses Android's native BiometricPrompt system. AutoLog never stores or accesses raw biometric credentials.
  • Local Storage & Exports: All trip entries and settings stay on your device in a local SQLite database. Exported CSV files or backups are generated locally and shared only when you explicitly invoke Android's native share sheet.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not operate remote servers or databases, nor do we integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or user-tracking frameworks. Your data is never sold, shared, or uploaded automatically.

4. Data Retention & Control

You retain full control to edit, reclassify, or delete individual trip records and saved locations inside the app at any time. Uninstalling AutoLog or clearing app storage immediately and permanently removes all saved data from your device.

5. Required App Permissions

Permission Purpose
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Calculates precise trip mileage distance and determines start/stop addresses.
ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION Allows continuous automatic trip recording when driving with the screen off.
BLUETOOTH_CONNECT Detects vehicle hands-free connection to start/stop automatic logging.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION Displays an active recording notification in the system tray during trip tracking.
