1. About Claimr

Offline-First Tax Compliance Companion

Claimr is a modern, private, offline-first tax compliance and receipt management utility engineered specifically for salaried employees, independent contractors, commission earners, and individuals tracking medical and home-office tax deduction claims.

Built for accuracy and audit peace of mind, Claimr eliminates lost paper till slips, faded thermal receipts, and chaotic shoebox tax filing by turning receipts into categorized, audit-ready digital PDF binders.

2. Key Features

💼 Commission Deductions

Track allowable business deductions against commission income with custom expenditure categories and SARS-compliant calculations.

🏥 Medical Tax Credits

Log out-of-pocket medical bills, pharmacy gaps, and un-reimbursed claims for accurate Medical Scheme Fees Tax Credit calculation.

🏠 Home Office Claims

Calculate space apportionment ratios and deduct qualifying utilities, cleaning, fibre, and equipment repairs effortlessly.

✨ Gemini AI Scanner

Intelligent receipt extraction that instantly transcribes vendor names, totals, VAT, dates, and itemized invoice details.

📄 Multi-Page PDF Engine

Merge long thermal till slips and multi-page invoices into crisp, high-contrast, audit-ready PDF document packets.

🔒 Local Storage & Backups

Everything stays securely on your device with complete encrypted local ZIP backup and restore capabilities.

3. Privacy Policy & Principles

Your privacy is our core engineering principle. Claimr does not require an account, does not harvest personal details, and does not operate any cloud database storing your financial records. Your financial data belongs strictly to you.

3.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All user information and financial assets remain exclusively on your device within sandboxed application storage:

  • Expense Records: Amounts, dates, vendor names, expense categories, tax years, and claim notes.
  • Images & Receipts: Scanned slips, photo captures, attached receipts, and synthesized PDF documents.
  • User Settings: Tax year selections, custom categories, and calculation parameters.
  • Backups: Encrypted local ZIP files generated only when you choose to export or back up your records.

3.2 Use of Google Gemini AI (OCR Processing)

Claimr includes an optional AI-powered receipt scanner that uses Google's Gemini Vision API:

  • On-Demand Only: Optical Character Recognition (OCR) is triggered only when you explicitly press the AI scan button on a receipt image.
  • Ephemeral Processing: Receipt images are securely transmitted to Google Gemini solely to extract text fields (vendor name, total, date, VAT).
  • No Tracking or Profiling: Processed documents are not retained to train public AI models or to profile your purchasing habits.
  • Offline Fallback: You can always choose to manually enter expenses without using the AI scanner.

3.3 In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Subscriptions and premium upgrades (Claimr Pro) are processed securely through the official Google Play Billing system. Claimr does not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, banking information, or payment credentials.

3.4 Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transmit your personal or financial data to third parties. Claimr does not integrate third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or behavioral analytics trackers.

3.5 Data Control, Backup & Permanent Deletion

  • Full User Control: You can edit or delete any expense record, attached image, or category at any time inside the app.
  • Exporting: You can export your data as CSV spreadsheets or audit-ready PDF/ZIP archives via the native Android system share dialog.
  • Permanent Erasure: Uninstalling the application or clearing app data in Android system settings permanently and irreversibly deletes all stored records, receipts, and images from your device.
